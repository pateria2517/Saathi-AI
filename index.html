Understand Your Payslip & Check for Anomalies

Warehouse and factory workers can audit their monthly deductions against statutory EPFO (EPF Scheme 1952) and ESIC (ESI Act 1948) rules. If your payslip shows deductions that never reach your passbook, Saathi AI flags the issue neutrally and helps raise an HR ticket with your consent.
